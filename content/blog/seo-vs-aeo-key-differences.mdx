Traditional SEO focuses on helping websites rank in the top ten search results on Google, while Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) ensures artificial intelligence assistants recommend your business when users ask for solutions. 

While SEO measures rankings, traffic clicks, and keywords, AEO measures citation share, entity confidence, and direct recommendations. Founders who understand the technical differences between these two channels can capture high-intent buyers long before their competitors realize the rules of search have changed.

## What are the 5 core differences between SEO and AEO?

Understanding how AI assistants evaluate businesses requires examining five technical distinctions between traditional web search and modern answer engines.

### 1. From Keyword Matches to Semantic Entity Graphing
In traditional SEO, pages are built around target keywords and search volume. If a page mentions *"commercial real estate advisor Gurgaon"* in the H1 tag and body copy, search crawlers index it for that phrase.

In AEO, large language models do not care about keyword repetition. Instead, they examine whether your company is registered as a recognized **Entity** in knowledge graphs, cross-referencing your tax identifier, founder names, corporate office addresses, and verified accreditations across the web.

### 2. From Clicks to Synthetic Direct Answers
Google's business model historically relied on showing ads around blue links, incentivizing users to click multiple pages to find an answer.

AI assistants operate with the opposite goal: they aim to provide a definitive, zero-click answer immediately. A user asking ChatGPT for a financial auditor does not want to read ten 2,000-word blog posts; they want two vetted firm names with reasons for the recommendation.

### 3. From Backlink Quantity to Cross-Web Consensus
A website with hundreds of low-quality guest post links might still manipulate traditional search algorithms temporarily. 

In contrast, LLMs rely on retrieval engines that look for **cross-web consensus**. If three independent, reputable publications (such as The Economic Times, an accredited bar council directory, or verified Google Business reviews) corroborate your firm's standing, the AI model gains high statistical confidence in naming you.

| Feature | Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) | Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Page rank and organic website visits | Being named in AI recommendations |
| **Primary Algorithm** | PageRank, link graphs, keywords | LLM neural weights & RAG retrieval |
| **Search Format** | Short keyword strings (2–4 words) | Natural conversational prompts (10–30 words) |
| **Target Medium** | Google, Bing, Yahoo Search | ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude |
| **Winning Position** | Top 10 organic positions | Top 1–3 cited entities |

## How does buyer behavior shift with Answer Engines?

When buyers in India migrate from typing keywords to asking conversational questions, their purchase intent is typically much further along:

1. **They provide complex constraints upfront:** Instead of searching *"best IVF clinic"*, they ask *"Which clinic in South Mumbai has the highest success rate for women over 40 and offers transparent payment plans?"*
2. **They accept the AI's top recommendations as pre-vetted:** Users treat ChatGPT and Gemini like knowledgeable colleagues rather than advertising billboards.
3. **They reach out directly:** Buyers who discover a brand through an AI recommendation frequently proceed directly to WhatsApp or phone calls rather than browsing general web content.

## Conclusion: Combining SEO and AEO for Maximum Visibility

Businesses do not need to choose between SEO and AEO. A comprehensive search strategy maintains strong technical SEO foundations while investing aggressively in structured entity graphs, multi-directory citations, and digital PR that AI models ingest into their recommendation engines.
