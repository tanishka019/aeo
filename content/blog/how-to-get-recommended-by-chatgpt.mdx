To get your business recommended by ChatGPT, you must establish verified entity authority, secure citations across trusted third-party web registries, and structure your website content with explicit Schema.org markup. 

OpenAI's search models prioritize consensus over self-promotion. By signaling verified proof of your credentials and services across multiple authoritative channels, you ensure ChatGPT recognizes your brand when prospective clients ask for recommendations.

## How does ChatGPT decide which businesses to recommend?

When someone types a search prompt into ChatGPT (such as *"Who are the top boutique corporate law firms in Bengaluru for seed-stage startups?"*), the model does not merely query a single search index. It initiates a real-time retrieval process:

1. **Query Expansion:** The model expands the prompt into multiple search vectors covering firm specialties, locations, client case studies, and partner recognitions.
2. **Authority Filtering:** ChatGPT queries web results, explicitly prioritizing established trade directories, professional registries, and accredited media publications.
3. **Synthesis & Ranking:** The model extracts businesses that appear repeatedly across high-confidence sources and summarizes their key differentiators.

## The 3 Critical Signals ChatGPT Searches For

### 1. Cross-Verified Citations on Reputable Third-Party Platforms
ChatGPT places minimal weight on claims published solely on your own website. If you state on your homepage that you are the *"#1 dental surgeon in Mumbai"*, the model considers this unsubstantiated self-praise.

However, if your practice is independently cited on:
- Government medical council registries (MCI, State Dental Councils).
- Authoritative healthcare aggregators (Practo, Lybrate, Justdial).
- Local business listings with verified reviews.
- Regional press mentions and trade journals.

ChatGPT uses this multi-source agreement to recommend your business with high confidence.

### 2. Complete Schema.org Entity Graphing
Large language models read structured code much faster and more reliably than unstructured marketing copy. By embedding nested JSON-LD schema on your website, you provide ChatGPT with machine-readable verification of:
- Legal entity name and founding year.
- Exact street address, geo-coordinates, and operating hours.
- Verified credentials and degrees of key founders or practitioners.
- Specific service offerings and accepted payment types.

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Apex Dental Clinic",
  "telephone": "+91-98765-43210",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "104 MG Road",
    "addressLocality": "Bengaluru",
    "postalCode": "560001",
    "addressCountry": "IN"
  }
}
```

### 3. Clear, Structured Q&A Content
ChatGPT prefers citing content that directly answers specific customer queries in the first two sentences. Websites structured with clear question-based H2 headings, short paragraphs, and transparent pricing structures or service boundaries are far more likely to be quoted directly as verified sources.
